# Quillbyte Security and Compliance

## Data Protection
- All data is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.2 or newer.
- All data at rest is encrypted with AES-256.
- Customer data is stored in the EU (Frankfurt) or US (Virginia). The region is chosen at signup and cannot be changed later.

## Certifications
- SOC 2 Type II, audited annually
- ISO 27001 certified since 2023
- GDPR compliant, with a Data Processing Agreement available on request

## Access Control
- Two-factor authentication is available on all plans and required for admins on Pro and Enterprise.
- Roles: Owner, Admin, Member and Read-only.
- SAML single sign-on and SCIM provisioning are Enterprise features.
- Audit logs are kept for 12 months and are Enterprise only.

## Data Retention and Deletion
When an account is closed, customer data is deleted within 30 days. Backups are purged within 90 days. Customers can request immediate deletion by emailing security@quillbyte.example.

## Incident Handling
Quillbyte notifies affected customers within 72 hours of confirming a security incident. Status updates are posted at `status.quillbyte.example`.

## Vulnerability Disclosure
Researchers can report issues to security@quillbyte.example. Quillbyte acknowledges reports within 2 business days and runs a bug bounty program with rewards from $100 to $5,000 depending on severity.

## Uptime
- Starter and Pro: 99.9% target, no financial SLA
- Enterprise: 99.95% SLA with service credits of 10% of the monthly fee for each 0.1% below target
