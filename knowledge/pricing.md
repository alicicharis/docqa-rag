# Quillbyte Pricing

All prices are in USD and billed per month. Annual billing gives a 20% discount.

## Plans

### Starter - $29 per month
- Up to 5 users
- Pulse: 10 hosts included
- Trailhead: 5 GB logs per month
- Watchtower: 1 on-call schedule
- Community support via forum

### Pro - $149 per month
- Up to 25 users
- Pulse: 50 hosts included, anomaly detection
- Trailhead: 50 GB logs per month
- Watchtower: unlimited schedules
- Email support with 1 business day response time

### Enterprise - custom pricing
- Unlimited users
- Single sign-on (SAML) and audit logs
- Custom data retention
- Dedicated support engineer and 99.95% uptime SLA
- Optional single-tenant deployment

## Overage Pricing
- Extra Pulse host: $2 per host per month
- Extra Trailhead logs: $0.50 per GB
- Extra user: $5 per user per month

## Free Trial
Every new account gets a 14-day free trial of the Pro plan. No credit card is required to start. After the trial, accounts move to Starter unless the customer upgrades.

## Discounts
- Startups under 2 years old: 50% off Pro for the first year
- Non-profits and open source projects: 30% off any plan
- Discounts cannot be combined.

## Refunds
Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time and are not refunded for the current month. Annual plans can be refunded in full within 30 days of purchase.
