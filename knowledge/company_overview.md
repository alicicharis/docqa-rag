# Quillbyte Company Overview

## About Quillbyte
Quillbyte is a fictional software company founded in 2018 in Rotterdam, Netherlands. It builds developer tools for monitoring, logging and incident response. The company has about 140 employees across offices in Rotterdam, Austin and Singapore.

## Mission
Quillbyte's mission is to help small and mid-size engineering teams find and fix production problems in minutes instead of hours.

## Company Timeline
- 2018: Company founded, first prototype of Pulse released.
- 2020: Launched Pulse 1.0 and reached 500 customers.
- 2022: Released Trailhead, the log search product.
- 2024: Introduced Watchtower, the incident response product.
- 2025: Passed 4,000 customers and opened the Singapore office.

## Values
1. Ship small and ship often.
2. Be honest with customers, even about outages.
3. Documentation is part of the product.
4. Own your on-call.
